Privacy Policy

Localjoin is a local services marketplace where customers can discover providers and businesses, create requests, and manage account activity. This policy explains the types of information used by the platform and the choices available to users.

Information we collect

When you use Localjoin, we may process your name, email address, phone number, account role, profile image, and service-area details such as city, state, area and pincode. Providers and businesses may provide service descriptions, prices, contact details, availability, portfolio images, and business details. Customers may submit service requests, descriptions, location preferences and scheduling information. Reviews, messages sent through the contact form, favorites, reports, notifications, and relevant account activity may also be processed.

How we use information

We use account information to manage registration, login and email verification; display selected public profile details; connect customers with eligible service providers or businesses; maintain service-request and status histories; provide support; moderate reports and reviews; protect accounts from abuse; and operate billing or subscriptions when those features are available.

Visibility and sharing

Information placed in public provider or business profiles, such as business names, services, service areas, prices and published reviews, may be visible to visitors. Information included in a service request may be made available to the relevant provider or business so they can respond. Other information may be shared with infrastructure, hosting, email-delivery, storage or payment partners where required for the features you use. We may disclose information where required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

Location information

Localjoin primarily uses location or service-area fields entered by users. Latitude and longitude fields may also be saved when supplied; they are not a requirement for ordinary service discovery. Avoid placing unnecessary private address details in public profile descriptions.

Cookies and security

Essential session and security-related cookies help keep users signed in and protect forms. The application stores password hashes rather than plain-text passwords and uses access controls for restricted pages. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Do not share passwords or verification links with others.

Retention and account requests

Account, transaction, moderation and service-request records are kept for as long as reasonably necessary for platform operations, resolving disputes, security and applicable legal obligations. You may contact Localjoin to ask about access, correction or deletion of personal information. Some records may need to be retained where legally required or necessary to resolve legitimate issues. Requests will be assessed in accordance with applicable law.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions or account-data requests, use the Localjoin contact form. We may update this policy as features or requirements change. Material changes should be communicated through an appropriate platform notice.
